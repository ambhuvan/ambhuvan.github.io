Research

Research Manager & Lead — MARS Labs C-IoT, Bangalore · Jun 2025 – Present

UAV Surveillance & Autonomous Threat Response

Leading the design and execution of a multi-modal drone detection and response system at MARS Labs C-IoT. The system fuses RCNN-based visual perception with LiDAR point-cloud localization to produce precise (x, y, z) coordinate tracking of aerial targets across challenging real-world environments. A GRU-based Seq2Seq model forecasts drone flight paths 3–5 seconds ahead — enabling proactive threat assessment and autonomous countermeasure triggering well before reactive systems can respond.

As Research Manager, I run the full lifecycle of research at the lab: setting technical direction, defining project scopes, allocating resources, and holding teams accountable to timelines and publication standards. I supervise and mentor student researchers from initial hypothesis through experiment design, model evaluation, and final submission. I conduct structured performance reviews of all active projects, identify research dead-ends early, and redirect effort where it can have the most impact. I also evaluate and green-light funding decisions — assessing feasibility, novelty, and potential for real-world deployment before committing lab resources to new directions. Multi-source training data (DUT Anti-UAV, RIOTU Synthetic, UZH-FPV, Mid-Air) was curated and benchmarked under my oversight to maximize robustness across lighting conditions, drone types, and flight profiles.
